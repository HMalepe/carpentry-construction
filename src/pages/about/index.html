/02How we work

Four rules on every site.

These are the standards we hold ourselves to on every job, big or small.

  1. Rule 01

    Measure before pricing

    Quotes come from a site visit, your drawings or the issued BOQ — not guesswork.

  2. Rule 02

    Put it in writing

    Scope, price and changes are agreed in writing before the work is done.

  3. Rule 03

    Keep you posted

    Progress updates on WhatsApp, with photos, so you're never guessing.

  4. Rule 04

    Hand over clean

    Snags attended to, site cleaned, documents handed over.
