/02Process

How a job runs.

The same four stages whether it's a cupboard or a classroom block — so you always know what happens next.

SCOPE → HANDOVER
  1. 01 · Site measurement

    We see it first

    We visit the site, measure, photograph and confirm the scope with you — or work from your drawings.

  2. 02 · Bill of quantities

    Itemised pricing

    You get an itemised quote. For tenders and RFQs we price against the issued bill of quantities.

  3. 03 · Build & joinery

    Work on site

    Construction, repairs and carpentry carried out to the agreed scope, with progress updates.

  4. 04 · Handover

    Snag & close-out

    Joint inspection, snag list attended to, site cleaned and handed back with close-out documents.
