Questions

Carpentry FAQ

Short answers. Anything else, ask on WhatsApp.

Do you make built-in cupboards to measure?

Yes. We measure on site and quote with the materials and finishes listed. Send photos of the room and any ideas you like on WhatsApp to start.

Can you replace or repair a roof structure?

Yes — trusses, purlins and the sheeting on top. Send photos of the roof (inside and out if you can) so we can see what we're dealing with.

Do you do carpentry for schools and public buildings?

Yes: doors and frames, shelving, ceilings and roof work for public facilities. See our Tenders & Compliance page.

Do you build decks and pergolas for lodges?

Yes. Timber decks, walkways and shade structures. Tell us the size and location and send photos of the spot.
