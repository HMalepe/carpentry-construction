Questions

Asked often.

Can you build from my architect's plans?

Yes. Send the drawings on WhatsApp — PDFs or clear photos of each sheet — and we'll price from them. If the plans aren't approved yet, tell us; that affects the programme.

Do you do government and municipal building work?

Yes. We quote on RFQs and tenders for public buildings. Compliance documents are available on request.

Do you work outside Hoedspruit?

We work across Maruleng and the surrounding area. Send your location pin on WhatsApp and we'll confirm.

How long will my build take?

It depends on the size of the job, approvals and material lead times. We give you a programme with the quote, once the scope is clear.
