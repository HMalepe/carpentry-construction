/02 · Pricing

How we price it

  • Planned maintenance: priced per cycle or per facility, against an agreed schedule.
  • Reactive repairs: quoted per job after we've seen the problem (photos help).
  • Public sector: priced against the specification or bill of quantities issued.

/03 · Before you message

What to send us

  • Photos or a short video of the problem
  • Facility name and location pin
  • For programmes: number of buildings and the maintenance period
  • Any condition report you already have

Everything above can be sent straight into the WhatsApp chat.

Questions

Maintenance FAQ

Short answers. Anything else, ask on WhatsApp.

Can we put our facility on a maintenance programme?

Yes. We agree a schedule — for example quarterly roof and gutter checks — and report back after each visit with what was found and repaired.

Can I send photos of the problem instead of booking a visit?

Yes, start there. Clear photos or a short video on WhatsApp often let us scope the job or tell you what to expect before a site visit.

Do you maintain government and municipal buildings?

Yes. See our Tenders & Compliance page for registration details and the company profile.
